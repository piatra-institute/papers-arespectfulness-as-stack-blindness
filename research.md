## §2-§3 — The concept and the mechanism of recession

- [T1] Working infrastructure becomes visible upon breakdown; its normal state is an embedded, transparent, learned-as-membership invisibility — Star (1999), *American Behavioral Scientist* 43:377; Star & Ruhleder (1996), *Information Systems Research* 7:111; Bowker & Star (1999). The sociological anchor; arespect relocates the phenomenon from a property of systems to a disposition of the perceiver.
- [T1] The ready-to-hand tool withdraws in skilled use and becomes conspicuous (present-at-hand) only when it breaks — Heidegger (1962), *Being and Time* §§15-16. The phenomenological root of stack blindness.
- [T1] Black-boxing: technical work is made invisible by its own success; the more science and technology succeed, the more opaque they become — Latour (1999), *Pandora's Hope*, glossary. The single most precise statement of the mechanism; quoted closely.
- [T1] Tacit knowing: we attend *from* subsidiary particulars *to* a focal whole, and focal attention on the subsidiary destroys the performance — Polanyi (1966), *The Tacit Dimension*. Supplies the from/through grammar and the key concession: attending-from is necessary and non-pathological; arespect is the slide from "I attend from this" to "there is nothing here."
- [T1] The technical object carries an internal genesis (individuation, concretization) to which culture is structurally blind — Simondon (2017), *On the Mode of Existence of Technical Objects*. Names the content that recedes.
- [T1] Tertiary retention: technical exteriorization of memory reaches each newcomer already complete, with forgetting built into transmission — Stiegler (1998), *Technics and Time, 1*. Grounds "arespect is the default, respect the achievement."
- [T2] Concept-distinction neighbors, each cited for one contrast in the §2 table: disenchantment — Weber (1946); reification — Lukács (1971); alienation — Marx (1959); inattentional blindness — Mack & Rock (1998), Simons & Chabris (1999); change blindness — Rensink et al. (1997); misplaced concreteness — Whitehead (1925).

## §4 — Breath as inherited geology

- [T2] **ESTABLISHED (cause); LIVE-CONTROVERSY (precise onset).** The Great Oxidation Event, the permanent rise of atmospheric O2 from oxygenic photosynthesis, onset ~2.4-2.3 Ga, diagnosed by loss of the sulfur mass-independent-fractionation signal; oxygenic photosynthesis predates accumulation by hundreds of Myr (the lag) — Lyons, Reinhard & Planavsky (2014), *Nature* 506:307 (anchor review); Bekker et al. (2004), *Nature* 427:117; Poulton et al. (2024), *Nature* 630:145 (coupled oxygenation ~2.3 Ga, protracted). Safe phrasing: a slow planetary process, not a datable event.
- [T2] **ESTABLISHED (mechanism); SPECULATION (mass extinction).** O2 and reactive oxygen species oxidize Fe-S clusters, iron centers, DNA; antioxidant defenses (SOD, catalase, peroxidases) evolved in response — Imlay (2003), *Annu. Rev. Microbiol.* 57:395; Imlay (2013), *Nat. Rev. Microbiol.* 11:443. The "oxygen catastrophe / mass extinction of anaerobes" framing lacks direct evidence — Sessions et al. (2009), *Current Biology* 19:R567. Safe phrasing: selective pressure and niche restriction, never "mass extinction."
- [T1] **PLAUSIBLE-INFERENCE (anaeroby = bedrock; vent metabolism contested).** LUCA reconstructed as anaerobic, H2-dependent, CO2-fixing (Wood-Ljungdahl), thermophilic, hydrothermal — Weiss et al. (2016), *Nature Microbiology* 1:16116; revised toward ~4.2 Ga and a larger gene set by Moody et al. (2024), *Nat. Ecol. Evol.* 8:1654. The paper asserts only anaeroby; the vent metabolism is flagged as the Weiss hypothesis.

## §5 — The engine in the cell

- [T1] **ESTABLISHED (single origin); LIVE-CONTROVERSY (early/late timing).** Mitochondria from a single endosymbiosis of an alphaproteobacterium in an archaeal host — Margulis [Sagan] (1967), *J. Theor. Biol.* 14:225; sister to known Alphaproteobacteria — Muñoz-Gómez et al. (2022), *Nat. Ecol. Evol.* 6:253. Timing debate: hydrogen hypothesis — Martin & Müller (1998), *Nature* 392:37; Asgard archaea — Spang et al. (2015), *Nature* 521:173; cultivated syntrophic Asgard archaeon — Imachi et al. (2020), *Nature* 577:519.
- [T1] **ESTABLISHED.** Chemiosmosis: proton-motive force across the inner membrane drives ATP synthase — Mitchell (1961), *Nature* 191:144. Cristae shape (set by MICOS and ATP-synthase dimer rows) organizes the respiratory chain and tunes efficiency — Cogliati et al. (2013), *Cell* 155:160; Cogliati, Enriquez & Scorrano (2016), *Trends Biochem. Sci.* 41:261.
- [T1] **LIVE-CONTROVERSY.** Energy-per-gene thesis: mitochondria removed a bioenergetic ceiling and enabled eukaryotic complexity — Lane & Martin (2010), *Nature* 467:929. Directly rebutted: per-gene cost scales with cell volume in both domains, no decisive mitochondrial advantage — Lynch & Marinov (2015), *PNAS* 112:15690; energy-first eukaryogenesis questioned — Booth & Doolittle (2015), *PNAS* 112:10278. The paper never asserts the thesis without its rebuttal.
- [T1/T2] **ESTABLISHED (enabling-condition); SPECULATION (consciousness).** Brain ~2% mass, ~20% resting energy, stable across effort — Raichle & Gusnard (2002), *PNAS* 99:10237; most energy on signaling (action potentials + postsynaptic currents) — Attwell & Laughlin (2001), *JCBFM* 21:1133; Harris, Jolivet & Attwell (2012), *Neuron* 75:762. Synaptic mitochondria supply ATP, buffer Ca2+, fuel local translation for plasticity — Devine & Kittler (2018), *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.* 19:63; Rangaraju, Lauterbach & Schuman (2019), *Cell* 176:73. Neuronal vulnerability and stress — Picard & McEwen (2018), *Psychosom. Med.* 80:141. **Guardrail:** energy is a predisposition for consciousness, not its correlate — Chen & Zhang (2021), *Front. Syst. Neurosci.* 15:648860; the variational "free energy" is information-theoretic, not metabolic — Friston (2010), *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.* 11:127 (a category error to conflate). Interoception at the seam — Craig (2002), *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.* 3:655; Barrett & Simmons (2015), *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.* 16:419.

## §6 — Fire and the externalized mind

- [T1] **PLAUSIBLE-INFERENCE; LIVE-CONTROVERSY (timing).** Cooking externalizes digestion and helped permit brain expansion — Wrangham (2009), *Catching Fire*; Wrangham et al. (1999), *Current Anthropology* 40:567; expensive-tissue trade-off — Aiello & Wheeler (1995), *Current Anthropology* 36:199. Secure early fire is isolated (Wonderwerk ~1 Ma — Berna et al. (2012), *PNAS* 109:E1215; Gesher Benot Ya'aqov ~790 ka — Goren-Inbar et al. (2004), *Science* 304:725), but *habitual* fire may be only ~400-300 ka — Roebroeks & Villa (2011), *PNAS* 108:5209, straining the timeline; strict brain-gut trade-off qualified — Navarrete, van Schaik & Isler (2011), *Nature* 480:91.
- [T1/T2] **ESTABLISHED (externalized memory, accounting origin); LIVE-CONTROVERSY (Great Divide).** Writing began as token accounting — Schmandt-Besserat (1992), *Before Writing*; orality/literacy as externalized memory — Ong (1982); Goody (1977); Goody & Watt (1963), *CSSH* 5:304. The "literacy restructures consciousness" Great-Divide thesis is contested — Street (1984), *Literacy in Theory and Practice*.
- [T1] Plato's *Phaedrus* (1995, Nehamas & Woodruff trans.), 274c-275b: writing as a pharmakon producing forgetfulness, an external reminding rather than memory. A 2,400-year-old statement of arespect at the moment of a layer's installation.
- [T2] **ESTABLISHED.** Cumulative culture / the ratchet: capability accumulates across generations beyond any single mind — Tomasello (1999), *The Cultural Origins of Human Cognition*; no individual can rebuild the stack, evidenced by well-equipped explorers starving for lack of local know-how — Henrich (2016), *The Secret of Our Success*.

## §7 — The designed black box

- [T1] **ESTABLISHED.** Early compiling systems met with disbelief — Hopper (1952), *Proc. ACM National Meeting*; a compiler rivaling hand assembly against efficiency skepticism — Backus et al. (1957), *Proc. Western Joint Computer Conference*.
- [T1] Abstraction creates a new semantic level at which one can be absolutely precise — Dijkstra (1972), *CACM* 15:859 (Turing lecture). Information hiding: deliberate concealment of a module's internals behind an interface — Parnas (1972), *CACM* 15:1053 (stack invisibility as an engineering requirement). Near-decomposable hierarchic systems and the watchmakers parable — Simon (1962), *Proc. Am. Philos. Soc.* 106:467. Leaky abstractions: all non-trivial abstractions leak — Spolsky (2002), *Joel on Software* (the engineered breakdown-reveals-the-stack). Essential versus accidental complexity — Brooks (1987), *Computer* 20:10.

## §10 — Maintenance and the distributive edge

- [T1] Maintenance labor is rendered invisible by the cult of innovation — Russell & Vinsel (2016), *Aeon*; Vinsel & Russell (2020), *The Innovation Delusion*. Use-centered history shows maintenance dominates technological effort yet is barely studied — Edgerton (2007), *The Shock of the Old*. Grounds the §10 claim that arespect toward the stack is continuous with arespect toward its maintainers.

## What was deliberately left out

- The seed's biological gestures were re-grounded and weakened where popular framing overreaches: no "oxygen holocaust / mass extinction of anaerobes" (Sessions et al.), no claim that LUCA "lived at vents" (only anaeroby), no claim that mitochondria "made complexity possible" (Lane & Martin always paired with Lynch & Marinov), and emphatically no claim that cristae "produce consciousness" (enabling-condition only; Chen & Zhang's own predisposition framing).
- The variational-versus-metabolic free-energy conflation is marked and refused rather than exploited.
- The seed's long enumerations (the dozen "stacks") are compressed to the load-bearing cases (oxygen, mitochondria, fire, writing, compilers) so each can be evidenced rather than listed.
- No empirical "stack depth" is quantified; the paper is conceptual, and its numbers are sourced dates and energy budgets, never a fabricated index.
